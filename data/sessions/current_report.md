## Key Concerns & Red Flags

*   **Extreme Fire Hazard:** The combination of Frame construction (Class 1) and the use of hazardous industrial solvents in manufacturing presents an exceptionally high fire and explosion risk, evidenced by a $220,000 solvent fire claim in 2023.
*   **Inadequate Fire Protection:** Only 60% partial dry sprinkler system and a local-only fire alarm system are insufficient for a high-hazard, 24/7 manufacturing occupancy.
*   **Flood Vulnerability:** Despite being in Flood Zone X, the property experienced a flash flood event in 2021, indicating a specific site vulnerability.
*   **Aging Infrastructure:** The 49-year-old frame building features critical systems well past their typical service life, including 26-year-old plumbing and a 19-year-old flat gravel roof, increasing maintenance and failure risks.
*   **Significant Loss History:** Four claims totaling $380,000 over the past five years, encompassing fire, equipment breakdown, flood, and theft, suggest systemic risk management deficiencies.
*   **Continuous Operations:** 24/7 operations inherently extend exposure time to all identified risks.

---

## Recommendations

*   **Fire Protection Upgrade:** Implement full (100%) wet sprinkler system coverage throughout the facility. Install a central station monitored fire alarm system.
*   **Hazardous Material Management:** Conduct an immediate and comprehensive review of hazardous material storage, handling, and disposal protocols. Implement specialized fire safety training and emergency response drills specific to solvent hazards.
*   **Infrastructure Renovation:** Prioritize the replacement of the entire plumbing system and the flat gravel roof to mitigate water damage and structural integrity concerns.
*   **Flood Mitigation Study:** Commission a detailed site-specific flood risk assessment and drainage study. Implement recommended engineering controls and emergency preparedness plans based on findings.
*   **Security Enhancement:** Review and upgrade current security measures beyond the basic alarm system, especially given the prior theft claim.

---

## Underwriting Decision

**REFER TO SENIOR REVIEW / DECLINE** — Given the "VERY HIGH" overall risk profile, the confluence of hazardous manufacturing operations, vulnerable frame construction, inadequate fire protection, significant and recent loss history (including major fire and flood), and aging critical infrastructure, this property poses an exceptionally high and potentially uninsurable risk under standard terms. A referral is mandatory to explore if any stringent risk improvement requirements, specialized sub-limits for hazardous materials, very high deductibles, or specific policy exclusions could make this account acceptable, or if a declination is warranted due to the inherent and unmitigated hazards.
